# Chapter 7 : Multimedia

## Introduction to Multimedia

- Multimedia may be defined as a presentation in which two or more media perform simultaneously where the media can be text, graphics, audio, animation, video and data.
- It is the field concerned with the computer-controlled integration of such media, in which every type of information is processed digitally.
- The principal theme underlying the use of multimedia is **interactivity**.
- Formerly confined to computer games, multimedia is now implemented widely across different fields owing to the advancement of technology and processing capability.

---

## Components of Multimedia

The components of multimedia are text, graphics or image, audio, video and animation.

### Text

- Text may consist of a word, a single line or a paragraph, and is the most convenient medium for conveying a message.
- It is developed using a text editor or word processing software; graphics software is required for special effects.
- Text may be varied in type, size, colour and style to suit professional requirements.

### Graphics or Image

1. Graphics refers to an image or a picture, which expresses ideas more effectively than text.
2. Unlike text, which follows the universal ASCII format, graphics do not conform to a single agreed format.
3. A computer image is formed of pixels, or dots, arranged upon the screen.
4. **Resolution** is determined by the pixel density and the number of colours, and the size of an image depends upon it.
5. Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator and Paint Shop Pro are examples of graphics packages, which also compress images to occupy less disk space.

### Audio

1. Audio enhances the usability of multimedia to its full potential and is of the utmost importance when video and animation are employed.
2. It includes human voices, instrumental notes and natural sound, used in any meaningful combination.
3. Audio is converted into digital format for use in multimedia and re-converted into analog form for output through the speakers.
4. It provides commentary without obscuring the screen and conveys a sense of place or mood.

### Video

1. Video is the running of frames of static pictures, each frame being read by the computer as a bitmap image.
2. A video clip consists of a series of still pictures called **frames**, each differing slightly from the one preceding it.
3. A rate of fifteen to twenty-five frames per second is maintained so that the movement appears smooth.
4. Video encompasses a raster graphics formation and is among the most widely used components of multimedia.

### Animation

1. Computer-based animation is a sequence of still vector images displayed in rapid succession to provide visual effects.
2. It is used to express ideas requiring complex explanation, as moving images exert an overpowering effect upon human peripheral vision.
3. Animation may be two-dimensional or three-dimensional, and imparts life to computer-generated images.
4. Animator Pro, Maya 3D, AutoCAD, 3DMax and Macromedia Flash are used to create animation.
5. It is widely applied in film-making, eg. Titanic.

## Application of Multimedia

Multimedia is profoundly expressed in the following areas.

### 1. Education

- Multimedia presentation enables a concept to be grasped with ease, unlike lectures alone.
- E-books such as electronic encyclopedias and electronic dictionaries are available.
- Assists medicine in diagnosis, scientific research in investigation, and the interpretation of engineering drawings.

### 2. Entertainment

- Widely adopted by the entertainment industry through high-resolution graphics and animation.
- Used in world-renowned films such as I-Robot, Terminator and Titanic.

### 3. Web Page

- Early web pages used text alone and were static in nature.
- Graphics and animation now render websites dynamic and eye-catching.

### 4. Business

- Marketing advertisements convince customers regarding products and services.
- Companies elaborate upon product features in an easily understandable manner.

### 5. Office Automation

- Meetings, discussions, conferences and seminars are core to strategic planning.
- Conducted through video conferencing, audio conferencing and document sharing, even where participants are geographically dispersed.

### 6. Communication

- Chatting was formerly confined to text alone.
- It is now conducted through video chatting and audio chatting.

## Advantages and Disadvantages of Multimedia

### Advantages

1. It increases the effectiveness of learning.
2. It is more appealing than traditional lecture-based methods.
3. It offers significant potential for improving personal communication, education and training.
4. It reduces the cost of training.
5. It is easy to use.
6. It provides high-quality video images and audio.

### Disadvantages

1. It is expensive.
2. It is not always easy to configure.
3. It requires special hardware.
4. It is not always compatible.
